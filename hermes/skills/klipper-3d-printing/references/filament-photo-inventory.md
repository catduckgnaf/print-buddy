# Filament photo inventory

Every direct photo attachment that depicts a filament spool, filament box, or its label is an instruction to inventory it in Spoolman, not merely an invitation to admire another circle of plastic. Inspect the label, search existing Spoolman records first, then create or update the vendor, filament, and spool through the direct Spoolman API. Use Moonraker's `/server/spoolman/spool_id` for the active spool and the LAN Spoolman REST service for inventory details and writes. Do not wait for or restore a Spoolman MCP. Record only facts visible on the label or unambiguously stated by the operator: brand, material, color, diameter, nominal mass, remaining mass if legible, and useful label metadata such as batch or temperature range. Attach the source image when the Spoolman API supports it. Do not create duplicates. If a required field, especially nominal weight, cannot be read or reasonably determined, ask only for that missing field; do not invent it. Do not treat printer-camera images, a print, or loose unlabelled filament as a spool inventory event.
